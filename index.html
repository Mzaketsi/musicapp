4. Create your generation brief

This prototype prepares a detailed instruction for a future connected AI music engine. It does not generate AI audio yet.

Beat studio (make your own song)

Tap the squares to place drums and notes, then press Play. Pick a key and scale and every note stays in tune. Make up to four patterns (A to D), then line them up into a full song. Record your voice over the beat, and download your work as WAV or MIDI. The sounds are made inside the app.

Keys and melody

Real sampled sounds come from the FluidR3 GM SoundFont through the midi-js-soundfonts project. Check its licence terms (attribution is required) before you release music that uses them.

Mix your voice (effects)

Record your voice in the Studio card first. Then set reverb, echo and tone, preview the mix, and download it as a WAV file. Your voice is saved dry, so you can change the effects without singing again.

Record your voice first.

Reference analyser and piano roll

Upload a track or one stem. Choose what it is first. Bass and melody show together on the piano roll (gold = bass, green = melody). Drums show on their own lanes. Download MIDI for your DAW. Results are estimates, so check them by ear. Works best on one instrument at a time.

Stems and multitrack mixer

Split a song (under 25 MB) into vocals, drums, bass and other, or load stems you already have. Each track has volume, mute and solo, a WAV download and an Analyse button. Only use songs you have the rights to. Each minute of audio uses one StemSplit minute.
