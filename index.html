Create your next sound.

A music creation workspace designed around Amapiano, Maskandi, Gqom, Deep House and the sounds of South Africa.

1. Choose your sound

Select a genre. You can refine the sound in Advanced Studio.

2. Advanced production controls

Set the musical direction for the generation engine.

3. Voice workspace

Plan your vocal sound. Voice recording, cloning and conversion need a connected, consent-based voice engine.

This prototype does not upload or clone the selected recording. The consent check is a design safeguard for a future voice service.

Studio: beat and your voice

Pick a backing track (the built-in beat, an audio file from your phone, or your AI song), record your own voice over it, then play back or download the mix. Use headphones so the beat is not picked up by the mic.

Download recording

4. Create your generation brief

This prototype prepares a detailed instruction for a future connected AI music engine. It does not generate AI audio yet.

Mix your voice (effects)

Record your voice in the Studio card first. Then set reverb, echo and tone, preview the mix, and download it as a WAV file. Your voice is saved dry, so you can change the effects without singing again.

Record your voice first.

Reference track analyser

Upload a track (like an AI song you use as a reference). The app estimates its tempo, key, chords and bass notes. Download the bass line as a MIDI file to open in your DAW. These are estimates, so check them by ear.

AI song (ElevenLabs)

Turns your brief into a real song with vocals. Each song uses ElevenLabs credits (about 1,800 per minute on the free plan), so make one at a time.
